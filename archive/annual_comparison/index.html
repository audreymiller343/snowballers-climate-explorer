READING THE CHART

Change relative to a recent past

Zero is this model’s mean temperature over 1995–2014. A value of +2 °C means two degrees warmer than that baseline, not two degrees above preindustrial conditions. The gray line is a historical simulation; colored lines are future scenario runs.

Annual variability is visible on purpose. Compare sustained differences across years, rather than treating a single year as a trend.

METHOD & SCOPE

One model, three scenarios

Selected CMIP6 model · monthly near-surface air temperature (tas). Annual means weight months by their calendar days. Each grid cell’s 1995–2014 annual mean is subtracted before spatial averaging.

Regional means use spherical latitude-strip areas from the model’s published cell bounds, with fractional overlap at band edges. Bands include land and ocean. These conditional projections represent one realization, with no estimate of model or ensemble uncertainty.
